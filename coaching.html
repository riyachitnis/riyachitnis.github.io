How it helps

Start with the point of friction.

Choose the pattern that feels most familiar. Coaching helps us understand it, reduce the hidden load and test a more workable response.

01 · Starting

“I want to do it. Why can’t I begin?”

We uncover hidden steps and create a smaller, clearer entry point.

02 · Time

“The day disappears before I catch up.”

We make time visible through realistic cues, anchors and transitions.

03 · Overwhelm

“Everything feels urgent.”

We unload the noise, sort what matters and choose one next action.

04 · Consistency

“My routines work until they don’t.”

We build flexible versions and make restarting part of the system.

No perfect week required.

Before we begin

Useful things to know.

Still unsure? The first conversation is free and there is no pressure to continue.

What is ADHD coaching?

A collaborative, action-oriented process focused on present-day goals, patterns and practical experiments.

Do I need an ADHD diagnosis?

No. Coaching does not assess or diagnose ADHD. You are welcome if attention, organization or follow-through is getting in the way.

Do you coach children and teenagers?

Yes. Coaching is adapted to the young person’s age, needs and goals. A parent or legal guardian takes part in the initial conversation and agrees to the coaching arrangement for anyone under 18.

Is this therapy or medical care?

No. Coaching is not psychotherapy, counselling, diagnosis or medical treatment. If your needs are clinical or urgent, contact an appropriately licensed professional.

Where do sessions happen?

Online worldwide, or in person in Abu Dhabi in a mutually agreed setting.

What happens in the free conversation?

We spend 20 minutes discussing what feels difficult, what you want to change and whether this approach is a useful fit. For clients under 18, a parent or legal guardian joins this conversation.
