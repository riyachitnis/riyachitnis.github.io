Why Clearer Days

Support that sees the whole person.

I have spent much of my life alongside people with ADHD, listening to what is difficult, noticing what helps and seeing how easily capable people can be misunderstood.

Five years of medical education and clinical placements shaped the way I listen, explain complex ideas and stay calm when life feels overwhelming. My coaching brings that foundation together with practical problem-solving, warmth and an understanding of the pressures people face at different ages.

I am not here to make you perform a more “normal” version of yourself. I am here to help you build days that are clearer, kinder and more workable for the brain you have.

Clear boundaries

Coaching is practical support, not clinical care.

Clearer Days does not diagnose ADHD, provide psychotherapy or counselling, prescribe medication or treat mental health conditions.

If your needs are clinical or urgent, I will encourage you to contact an appropriately licensed professional. If you may harm yourself or someone else, contact local emergency services immediately.
