Meet Riya

I understand the gap between caring deeply and still feeling stuck.

I have spent much of my life alongside people with ADHD—listening to what is difficult, noticing what helps and understanding how easily capable people can be misunderstood.

Five years of medical education and clinical placements shaped the way I listen, explain complex ideas and stay calm when life feels overwhelming. My coaching brings that foundation together with practical problem-solving, warmth and an understanding of the pressures adults face today.

Before we begin

Useful things to know.

If you are unsure whether coaching is the right kind of support, send me a message. The first conversation is free and there is no pressure to continue.

What is ADHD coaching?

It is a collaborative, action-oriented process. We focus on present-day goals, patterns and practical experiments that can support planning, follow-through, routines and self-understanding.

Do I need an ADHD diagnosis?

No. Coaching does not assess or diagnose ADHD. You are welcome if attention, organization or follow-through is getting in the way and you want practical support.

Is coaching the same as therapy or medical care?

No. Coaching is not psychotherapy, counselling, diagnosis or medical treatment. I do not prescribe medication or treat mental health conditions. If your needs are clinical or urgent, I will encourage you to contact an appropriately licensed professional.

Where do sessions happen?

Online sessions are available worldwide. In-person sessions in Abu Dhabi can be arranged in a mutually agreed, appropriate setting.

What happens in the free conversation?

We spend 20 minutes discussing what feels difficult, what you would like to change and whether my approach is a useful fit. It is not a diagnosis and there is no obligation to book.

Who is coaching not suitable for?

Coaching is not crisis care and is not a replacement for clinical support. If you may harm yourself or someone else, contact local emergency services or a qualified crisis service immediately.
